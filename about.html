About Us

We are a small independent Kiwi owned and operated electric bike company based in Auckland. Dragoon Bikes was formed to give Kiwi customers the opportunity to purchase high quality bikes without having to pay the earth for it. If you are in need of a bike, you have come to the right place.

Make an informed decision: We believe a bike is more than just the frame, components, and wheels. Having knowledgeable people who know their stuff, and who make sure it is the right choice and fit for you, is far more important than getting the cheapest deal. We operate online, but you are welcome to ask our friendly staff any questions you may have. If you would like to view an item before you buy, we are happy to organise this.

Peace of mind: We know purchasing an electric bike can be a meaningful investment and we know trust is key for this experience. We want you to feel we have your back and it is our aim to earn your confidence. We stand behind our products and will endeavour to sort out any issues that may arise as soon as possible.
