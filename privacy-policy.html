Privacy Policy

Overview

Dragoon Bikes respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information submitted through our website, including booking forms, checkout, and direct enquiries.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, booking details, delivery notes, and order information when you use our website, request a test ride, or begin a checkout.

How We Use Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, arrange test rides, process checkout requests and payments, contact you about your order, and improve our services.

Payments

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. We do not store full card details on our website servers.

Third Parties

We may use third-party providers such as Stripe, hosting providers, and analytics or infrastructure vendors to operate the website and fulfil customer requests.

Data Retention

We retain customer and enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide service, comply with legal obligations, and maintain business records.

Contact

If you have privacy-related questions, please contact Dragoon Bikes directly via the contact details listed on the website.
